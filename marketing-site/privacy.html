Privacy Policy

Better Clover KDS  ·  Last updated: November 2024

This Privacy Policy explains how Better Clover KDS ("the App", "we", "us", "our") handles information when you use our kitchen display application. We are committed to transparency and to protecting the privacy of our users.

1. Information We Collect

Better Clover KDS collects the minimum information needed to function:

  • Clover OAuth Access Token — When you connect your Clover merchant account, the App stores an authentication token locally on your device. This token is used exclusively to communicate with Clover's API and is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Order and Menu Data — The App retrieves order tickets and related data from Clover's API in real time for display purposes. This data is not stored persistently by the App beyond your active session.
  • Device Information — Basic device identifiers (device model, operating system version) may be collected for crash reporting and app performance diagnostics.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used solely to:

  • Authenticate and connect to your Clover POS merchant account
  • Retrieve and display kitchen order tickets in real time
  • Maintain your session so you remain connected across app launches
  • Diagnose crashes and improve the App's performance and stability

We do not use your information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or any purpose unrelated to providing the kitchen display functionality.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your Clover credentials and access tokens are stored locally on your device using secure, OS-managed storage (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). We do not operate external servers that store your personal or business data. Order data is fetched live from Clover and is not written to any persistent database outside your device.

4. Third-Party Services

Better Clover KDS integrates with:

  • Clover POS (Fiserv) — The App connects to the Clover API to authenticate your account and retrieve order data. Your use of Clover is governed by Clover's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, available at clover.com/privacy-policy.

We do not share, sell, or otherwise disclose your data to any other third parties.

5. Data Retention

We do not retain your data on external servers. Any data stored locally on your device — such as your Clover access token or session preferences — can be removed by disconnecting your account within the App, or by uninstalling the App entirely. Uninstalling the App clears all locally stored data.

6. Children's Privacy

Better Clover KDS is a business tool intended for restaurant operators and kitchen staff. It is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information associated with your use of the App. Because we store data only locally on your device, you can exercise these rights at any time by clearing the App's local data or uninstalling the App. For any additional requests, please contact us directly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or applicable law. Any updates will be reflected with a revised date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after the effective date of any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
