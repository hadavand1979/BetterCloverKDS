Features

Everything your kitchen needs

Built for busy restaurant kitchens. No bloat — just orders, clear and fast.

🔢

Item tally on every ticket

A 10-item ticket can read 4 burgers, 2 steaks, and 10 fries. Sides are counted. Notes such as NO onion stay on the item.

✅

Mark each item done

Tap an item to cross it off. Tap it again to put it back. Bump still clears the whole ticket.

⏱️

Clock starts with the food

Drinks can open the check first. The timer starts when the kitchen items are rung in, then turns yellow at 5 minutes and red at 10.

🏷️

KDS printer label

Show only the items that carry your Clover printer label. Drinks without that label do not keep a finished food ticket open.

📋

Hold, bump, and closed

Hold parks a ticket. Bump moves it to Closed and freezes the time it took. Make active brings it back.

📱

Phone, tablet, or monitor

The same kitchen runs on iPhone, iPad, Android, and a touchscreen connected to a Mac, Windows PC, or Raspberry Pi.

Open the app

Connect Clover

In Settings, choose Clover account, United States and Canada, and enter the merchant ID and access token. Select the KDS printer label.
